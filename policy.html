Privacy Policy

This policy explains how AM Digital Studio handles information when you browse the website, create an account, manage a profile, place an order or contact us.

📅 Last updated: 28 November 2026•Applies to amdigitalstudio.site and its related pages
In brief: We use information to operate customer accounts, manage service and store orders, provide tracking and respond to messages. We do not ask you to send passwords or payment-card details through chat.

1. Who We Are and Scope

“AM Digital Studio”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the operator of the AM Digital Studio website and the digital services and store presented on it. The website is operated from Sri Lanka. This policy covers the website pages and related account, order, store and support features available through the website.

This page describes the practices reflected in the website’s current features. It is not a promise that every feature is available at all times.

2. Information We Collect

The information depends on which features you choose to use.

  • Account information: your name and email address when you register or sign in. Authentication is handled through Supabase Auth; the website does not need you to disclose your account password to us through a message.
  • Profile information: your profile name and optional profile picture. If you upload a picture, the image is stored in the website’s Supabase profile-photo storage and its image URL is saved with your profile.
  • Service order information: your name, email address, WhatsApp/contact number, country, selected service and package, project title and description, selected delivery option, quoted or estimated amount, and payment status or reference where applicable.
  • Store order information: your name, contact number, delivery address, optional note, selected products, sizes, colours, quantities and order total. Store orders are submitted by signed-in customers.
  • Tracking and account activity: order tracking numbers, order type, status, payment status, timestamps and information needed to display your own order history.
  • Messages you send: your name, email address, subject and message when you use the website contact form, and any information you choose to provide through support chat or WhatsApp.
  • Technical and browser information: information that your browser or service providers may process to deliver the website, protect services, remember preferences or keep the shopping cart available. The website also uses browser local storage for cart and temporary order-form details.

Please do not include passwords, full payment-card numbers, card security codes, banking login details or other highly sensitive information in a contact message, support chat or WhatsApp message.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create, authenticate and maintain customer accounts.
  • To show and update profile information and profile pictures.
  • To receive, review, manage and fulfil service and store orders.
  • To generate tracking numbers, show order progress and provide order history.
  • To contact you about an order, project requirements, payment confirmation, delivery or support request.
  • To respond to enquiries submitted through the contact form or support channels.
  • To maintain website functionality, troubleshoot errors, prevent misuse and protect the website and its users.
  • To remember selected preferences or cart/order-form information in your browser where the relevant feature uses local storage.

We do not describe the website as using Google Analytics or another analytics platform unless and until such a service is actually enabled.

4. When Information Is Shared with Other Services

Some website features depend on third-party providers. When you use those features, relevant information may be processed by the providers involved:

  • Supabase: used for account authentication, customer profile records, profile-photo storage and order data, including order tracking and order history.
  • EmailJS: used to transmit contact-form submissions, including the name, reply email, subject and message that you enter.
  • Chatway: a support-chat widget is loaded on the website. If you use it, information you submit through that chat may be processed by Chatway under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp: store checkout prepares an order message containing the details you enter and opens WhatsApp when you submit the form. You decide whether to send that message. WhatsApp then processes it under its own terms and privacy policy. Service-order support may also involve sending order details or payment proof through chat.
  • Website delivery providers: the site loads resources such as Google Fonts and Font Awesome from external content-delivery services. Those providers may receive technical request information such as your IP address and browser details when the resources load.
  • External destinations: links to YouTube, Trustpilot, social networks and other external websites take you to services that operate under their own privacy policies.

We do not control the independent privacy practices of these providers. Please review their current policies before submitting information to them.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect users or the website, investigate misuse, or enforce an agreement.

5. Where Information Is Stored and Security

Account, profile and order records are handled through the website’s Supabase project. Profile pictures are uploaded to the configured profile-photo storage bucket. Some temporary information, including the store cart and service-order form details, is held in your browser’s local storage.

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information. However, no website, network transmission, cloud service or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your account credentials private, use a unique password and sign out on shared devices.

Do not share your tracking number publicly. Anyone who obtains a tracking number may be able to request the corresponding tracking information through the tracking feature.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

The website uses browser local storage for functions such as retaining the store cart and temporarily carrying order details between pages. The website may also use storage or cookies required by the authentication, theme, chat or other embedded third-party features.

You can manage or clear browser storage and cookies through your browser settings. Clearing them may sign you out, remove your saved cart or erase unfinished order details. Blocking storage may prevent some features from working correctly.

7. How Long We Keep Information

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to operate accounts, handle orders and support requests, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, protect the website or meet applicable legal requirements. Retention periods may differ by information type and provider.

Removing a profile picture or changing account details does not necessarily remove information already included in an order, message, backup or third-party system. Where a deletion request is appropriate, we will assess it in light of operational, security and legal requirements.

8. Your Choices and Requests

  • You can update your profile name and upload or remove your profile picture through Account Settings, where those features are available.
  • You can use the account settings features to request a password reset or manage your account.
  • You can contact us to ask about personal information associated with your account or orders, or to request correction or deletion where appropriate.
  • You can choose not to use optional features such as support chat or the store. Some information is required to create an account or process an order.

To help us locate the relevant account, contact us from your registered email address where possible. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Certain records may need to be retained for legitimate operational, security or legal reasons.

9. Children and Young Users

The website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children who are not legally able to provide it independently. If you are under the age at which you can legally enter into a service or purchase agreement, involve a parent or legal guardian before creating an account, placing an order or providing personal information. If you believe a child has provided information inappropriately, contact us.

10. International Processing and Third-Party Policies

Some providers used by the website may process information on systems located outside Sri Lanka. Their handling of information is governed by their own terms, privacy notices and applicable safeguards. By choosing to use an embedded or linked third-party service, you may interact directly with that provider.

11. Applicable Privacy Law

We aim to handle personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable law. Sri Lanka’s Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022, has been amended by the Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2025. The commencement of provisions is subject to Gazette orders; the Data Protection Authority’s published implementation information should be consulted for the current commencement position. Nothing in this policy removes any rights or obligations that apply under law.

For official information, visit the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when the website, its providers, its features or applicable requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Please review it periodically.

13. Contact Us

If you have a privacy question or want to make a request about your information, contact AM Digital Studio through the Contact Us section on the website or through the available support chat. Please do not include passwords or payment-card security details in your message.
